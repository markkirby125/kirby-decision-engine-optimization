# Decision Engine Optimization (DEO) - Dispatcher

Every other AI SEO skill optimizes **discovery** (citations, impressions, top-of-funnel rankings). This skill optimizes the **decision stage**: the moment a corporate buyer, legal client, or homeowner pastes four competing quotes or an RFP PDF into ChatGPT/Claude and asks *"Which one provides the best value? Who should I place the order with?"*

**The economic case (V6-G — Dooley/Floate, "Entity SEO for AI Search", 2026; single 1.3M-inquiry study, directional):** AI-referred leads convert from cold inquiry to paying customer at **26%** — **3× standard web-search leads** and **2 points above word-of-mouth**, historically commerce's best channel (cross-industry: 1.3M inquiries, 850 industries; UTM-tracked from ChatGPT/AI search). Buyers arrive pre-sold — "where do I sign" — on perceived AI neutrality. *Harmonization note:* `kirby-off-page-seo` §4.21 carries a different-source figure for the same phenomenon (20–25% vs ~5% organic, Odys Global) — both are attributed; cite the range, never blend them into one number. Full benchmark: Module 1 §1.0.

When executing tasks under this skill, **do not guess**. Read the specific module below.

## When to Use
- A prospect's final decision step involves pasting competing quotes into an LLM ("we have 4 quotes — which should we choose?").
- You are structuring a sales proposal, PDF quote, or RFP response for LLM parseability (Module 5).
- You are auditing why an LLM recommends a competitor over the client (Module 1's reverse-audit prompts).
- You are deciding which contractual trust factors (insurance, ISO, accreditations) to disclose and at what precision (Module 2).
- An enterprise tender or procurement process requires verified entity status (Module 3).
- You are correcting LLM miscounts of the client's reviews across platforms, segmented by buyer persona (Module 4).

## How It Works
1. Confirm the decision scenario (competing quotes / tender / RFP) and the buyer persona.
2. Run Module 1's reverse-audit against the client and each competitor.
3. Patch gaps in order: Module 3 (entity gating) → Module 2 (contractual proof) → Module 4 (review proof) → Module 5 (document formatting).
4. Re-run the reverse-audit until the LLM argues *for* the client on every surfaced dimension.
