# Module 6: Third-Party Entity Ingestion — Seeding Consensus Matrices into LLM Retrieval

*Source: Charles Floate parasite-SEO masterclass (The Edward Show E1193) applied to the decision stage. Companion discovery-stage skills: `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` (placement execution), `../../kirby-technical-aeo/SKILL.md` (GitHub/Dev.to entity graphs).*

### **6.1 Why Third-Party Seeding Wins the Decision Query**
When a buyer pastes competing quotes into an LLM, the model reasons from whatever comparison structures it has already ingested — and **85% of AI brand citations for commercial queries originate from third-party domains**. A high-authority platform hosting a structured comparison matrix of your category becomes the "consensus fact" the LLM retrieves during the decision query. First-party comparison pages are discounted as self-reviews; the same matrix on GitHub, Reddit, or an industry publication is treated as neutral. This module seeds those surfaces deliberately.

### **6.2 Consensus Matrix Seeding Protocol**
1.  **Derive the matrix axes from Module 1:** The reverse-audit surfaces the dimensions buyers (or LLMs acting for them) actually weigh — price structure, SLA terms, accreditation, insurance limits, support model. The seeded matrix uses exactly those axes, not marketing dimensions.
2.  **Mirror 99%, differentiate 1%** (twin of the `kirby-aiseo-skill` Module 2 §2.40 comparison doctrine): the matrix reflects established category consensus, with the client positioned as superior only on checkable facts (price, SLA, accreditation) — never adjectives. A matrix that self-awards across every axis is discarded by consensus filters and reads as astroturf.
3.  **Facts must be verifiable and current:** every cell is either publicly checkable or certificate-backed (Module 2 micro-disclosure standard). Comparison claims that cannot be sourced are omitted, not smoothed.
4.  **Disclosure:** where platform rules or FTC guidance require it (sponsored or affiliated context), disclose — an undisclosed seeded matrix discovered by a buyer converts a decision asset into a trust liability.

### **6.3 Platform-Specific Ingestion Mechanics**
*   **GitHub:** comparison matrix as a repo README or standalone gist (markdown tables ingest cleanly into retrieval corpora and training scrapes). Pair with the repo entity-graph work in `../../kirby-technical-aeo/SKILL.md`. Maintain a `LICENSE` and change log so the repo reads as maintained infrastructure, not a landing page.
*   **Reddit:** standalone-value context posts (genuine "how to choose an X" with the matrix as reference material) in relevant subreddits — moderated UGC platforms remain open retrieval surfaces under the SRA boundary test (`../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 15 §15.3). Follow the Reddit mechanics in `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 13.
*   **Industry publications:** editorial comparison placements vetted against the newswire criteria (`../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 21 §21.1) — guaranteed indexation, self-canonical, retention history.

### **6.4 Freshness Loop**
LLM retrieval favors recently-updated sources and buyers notice stale pricing. Refresh every seeded matrix on a fixed cadence (quarterly default; monthly where pricing moves faster), with dated "last verified" rows. A dated, maintained matrix outlives the individual retrieval window; an abandoned one decays out of consensus.

### **6.5 Verification**
Probe the target LLMs monthly with the buyer-decision prompts from Module 1 ("compare [client] vs [competitors] on [axes]") and record whether the seeded matrix's framing surfaces in the answer. Consensus shift is slow (weeks to months — retrieval corpora lag publication); do not re-seed harder when a probe fails inside the lag window, verify indexation and refresh dates instead.
